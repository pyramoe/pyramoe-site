## Academic Focus

I’m an undergraduate student in Linguistics at Stockholm University. My interests span Afroasiatic, Semitic, and Indo-European languages; writing systems; phonetics and phonology; grammar; and historical linguistics. I’m especially interested in how language change works in real communities and how ideology, power, and identity shape what counts as “correct” language.
