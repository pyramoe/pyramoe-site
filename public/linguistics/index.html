Linguistics

Academic Focus

I’m an undergraduate student in Linguistics at Stockholm University. My interests span Afroasiatic, Semitic, and Indo-European languages; writing systems; phonetics and phonology; grammar; and historical linguistics. I’m especially interested in how language change works in real communities and how ideology, power, and identity shape what counts as “correct” language.

Competitions & Olympiads

  • Swedish Linguistics Olympiad — Finalist (2022, 2024)
  • International Linguistics Olympiad — Competitor for the Swedish National Team (2022, 2024)
  • International Linguistics Olympiad — Honorable Mention, Team Competition (2024)
  • International Linguistics Olympiad — Ethics Committee Member (2025–present)
  • Swedish Linguistics Olympiad — Organizer (2024–present), Board Member (2025–present)
